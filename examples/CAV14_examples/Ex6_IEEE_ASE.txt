## Example from "Formal Specification and Verification of Industrial Logic Components" IEE Trans. on Auto. Sc. and Engg. Vol. 7, No. 3, 2010

## Operation Specification
ASSUME ALWAYS (!(movefwd && movebwd))
ALWAYS (ALWAYS (movefwd -> EVENTUALLY (infwdpos || alarm)))
ALWAYS (ALWAYS (movebwd -> EVENTUALLY (inbwdpos || alarm)))

## Exception Specification
ALWAYS (ALWAYS emergencystop -> !(closevalve || openvalve))

## Invariants
ALWAYS (!(closevalve && openvalve))


INPUT  movefwd, movebwd, infwdpos, inbwdpos, emergencystop
OUTPUT closevalve, openvalve, alarm
