## Quiz game for 3 groups (each group has its special feature)
## Can use monolithic or compositional, with unroll 3

ALWAYS((!(x0 || x1) && !y1 && !y2 && NEXT (x0 || x1)) -> NEXT (y0 UNTIL x5))
ALWAYS((!x2 && !y0 && !y2 && NEXT x2) -> NEXT (y1 UNTIL x5))
ALWAYS((!(x3 && x4) && !y0 && !y1 && NEXT (x3 && x4)) -> NEXT (y2 UNTIL x5))

## Ensure that lights are not on unless someone pressed
((!y0 UNTIL (x0 || x1)) || (ALWAYS !(x0 || x1)))
((!y1 UNTIL x2) || (ALWAYS !x2))
((!y2 UNTIL (x3 && x4)) || (ALWAYS !(x3 && x4)))

## At most one light can be on
ALWAYS ((y0 && !y1 && !y2) || (!y0 && y1 && !y2) || (!y0 && !y1 && y2) || (!y0 && !y1 && !y2))

## When reset, all lights should be off until one or another answering event has happened
ALWAYS (x5 ->  (((!y0 && !y1 && !y2) UNTIL ((x0 || x1) || x2 || (x3 && x4))) || ALWAYS !((x0 || x1) || x2 || (x3 && x4))))

## From the simplified example, we realize that we are not allowed to have controllers to be triggered simultaneously.
## This implies that we are assuming that when the button is released, it is being set back to FALSE
ASSUME ALWAYS (x2 -> !x0)
ASSUME ALWAYS (x2 -> !x1)
ASSUME ALWAYS (x2 -> !x3)
ASSUME ALWAYS (x3 -> !x0)
ASSUME ALWAYS (x3 -> !x1)

## Reset comes once for a while
ASSUME ALWAYS <> x5
